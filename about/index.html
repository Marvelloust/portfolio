Personal codebook / how I read a build

A screen is only one layer of the evidence.

VISIBLECan someone understand what to do next?

Hierarchy, labels, state feedback, responsiveness and interaction should make the product legible before it becomes impressive.

UNDERNEATHWhat rule keeps the visible state true?

I look for the constraint behind the interface: availability, authorization, data consistency, retry behavior or integration state.

AFTER SHIPWhat happens when reality is messy?

Slow networks, repeated actions, missing data, wrong permissions and production fixes are part of the product record too.

Failure ledger / the states I look for

The interesting part often starts after the happy path.

These are not abstract UX concerns. They are the kinds of conditions that change how I structure a build.

SWIFTSLOT / REPEATCan one action become two bookings?

Idempotency, unique slot constraints and transaction boundaries keep a retry from becoming duplicated state.

FIELDNOTE / OFFLINECan work continue without a network?

Disconnected, queued and synchronized states need to be visible product states rather than hidden implementation details.

RELAY / CONTEXTIs the response grounded in the right record?

Support tooling needs customer context, source material and human review around the generated answer.

EVERY BUILD / MOBILEWhat survives when width disappears?

Hierarchy, actions, evidence and error recovery have to remain legible on the smallest useful screen.
